How this works

A structure check costs ~9 ns; a biome check ~31 µs — a ~3,650× gap. So the planner splits every structure condition into cheap 48-bit geometry (pass 1) and expensive biome viability (pass 2), and reorders by measured cost — not the order you wrote them.

Plan shows that ordering. Estimate samples the seed space and predicts whether the search will ever finish. Search runs it.
